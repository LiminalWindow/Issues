# Liminal Window — Research Archive

This repository serves as the permanent, version-controlled archive of publications issued through Liminal Window.

Originally established as an independent literary publication, Liminal Window has evolved into an ongoing research initiative dedicated to the study of poetry and poetic mechanisms within everyday language. Current publications focus on practice-based scholarship, literary observation, and the documentation of research conducted through the *Studies in Everyday Poetics* series.

For purposes of preservation and historical continuity, early literary publications remain part of the official archive, including *Issue No. 1* and the poem *The Vessel That Steered The Sea*. These works are retained as part of the publication's developmental record and are preserved alongside later research papers.

Each archived publication includes:

* The official publication file (PDF)
* A cryptographic timestamp (.ots)
* A verification record (TXT)

These materials establish:

* Authorship
* Publication date
* Document integrity

Publications are archived across multiple preservation repositories, including:

* Internet Archive
* Zenodo
* Figshare

All files contained within this repository are considered part of the official publication record of Liminal Window.

## Verification

Timestamp files (.ots) may be independently verified using OpenTimestamps to confirm that a document existed at or before its recorded timestamp.

## About

Liminal Window is an independent poetry research initiative created and maintained by Jaiden Hordosillo.

Through publication, archival preservation, and practice-based scholarship, the project investigates the ways poetic mechanisms operate throughout everyday life, language, culture, and communication.

Research publications are issued through the *Studies in Everyday Poetics* series and preserved as part of the permanent scholarly record.
